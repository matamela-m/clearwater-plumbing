Emergency Plumbing

A plumbing emergency is any situation that poses an immediate risk of water damage, health hazard, or loss of essential water supply. Common emergencies include:
  • Burst or rapidly leaking pipes
  • Flooding from any source
  • Completely blocked toilets (especially if only one in the home)
  • No water supply to the entire property
  • Sewage backup or overflow
  • Geyser failure in cold weather

If in doubt — call us. We'd rather come out and find it's minor than have you dealing with a flood alone.

While waiting for us to arrive, here's what to do:
  1. Turn off the main water supply — usually at the main stopcock near your water meter or under the kitchen sink.
  2. Turn off the electricity near the affected area if water is near electrical fittings.
  3. Move valuables away from the water if possible.
  4. Take photos for insurance purposes.
  5. Don't use any water until the plumber arrives and assesses the situation.

Your main water shut-off valve (stopcock) is usually located:

  • Under the kitchen sink
  • In a utility room or garage
  • Near the front boundary of your property (street-side water meter)

Turn it clockwise to close. If you can't find it, call us immediately — we can guide you over the phone while we're on the way.

After-hours emergency rates are from R750 for the call-out, compared to no call-out fee during standard business hours. The actual repair cost depends on the job. We always confirm the rate with you before dispatching, so there are no surprises. We believe emergencies deserve honest pricing — not price gouging.
