Our Story

From One Van to Centurion's Go-To Plumbers

Clearwater Plumbing was founded in 2009 by David van der Merwe, a certified master plumber with a simple mission: deliver honest, fast, and professional plumbing to the people of Centurion: without the runaround or hidden fees.

What started as a one-man operation with a single van has grown into a trusted team of PIRB-registered plumbers serving thousands of homes and businesses across Centurion and greater Gauteng. But our values haven't changed.

We still believe that every customer deserves a clear quote, a punctual plumber, and work they can rely on. That's why more than 60% of our business comes from returning customers and referrals.
